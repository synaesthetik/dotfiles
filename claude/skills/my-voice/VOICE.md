## Voice pointers

- Lowercase by default — messages often start lowercase, including the pronoun "i".
  Capitalization is relaxed, not enforced. e.g. "oh wait nvm" / "i am a n00b"
- Trailing ellipses mid-thought — "..." to trail off, pivot, or set up a beat,
  often mid-sentence. e.g. "need my off grid camper to be....moar off"
- Stretched-letter / maxed-number hyperbole for emphasis — "Sorrrryyyy", "WAYYY",
  "1000%". Intensity dialed past literal.
- Warm and generous with people — effusive, sincere praise and gratitude ("you're the
  best dude", "Heck yeah! These are great!", "Extreme gratitude"), plus friendly openers
  ("Hey folks"), smileys (:slightly_smiling_face:), and soft acks ("no sweat", "no prob!").
- Structures plans as ordered steps / bullets — multi-step technical reasoning is laid
  out as numbered lists in execution order, so others can follow and verify.
- Collaborative consent-seeking — proposes a direction, then hands the decision back.
  Closes with "does that sound ok?", "What are your thoughts?", "same page". Invites, doesn't dictate.
- Honest hedging on technical claims — flags uncertainty rather than feigning confidence.
  "I have a hunch", "Super random hail mary thought", "i think that sounds right?", ":shrugdog: idk".
- Vivid, homespun metaphors for technical concepts — carries the point through imagery,
  not jargon. "operator is none the wiser", "wool to be pulled", "die with the ship", "swamp gas".
- Owns mistakes openly, no defensiveness, often softened with humor — "Yeah this is
  definitely my mess". Rueful/sheepish ";/" (or ":/") marker. e.g. "Sorry again, ;/"
